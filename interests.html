05

Gunpla

I build Gunpla, the Gundam plastic model kits from Japan. Putting a kit together piece by piece from cutting parts off the frame to the final pose is relaxing in the same way coding is: follow the steps, fix your mistakes and end up with something you made yourself.

So far I have built High Grade (HG) kits, a 1/144 scale grade that is a good starting point for beginners. Here are a few from my collection.
